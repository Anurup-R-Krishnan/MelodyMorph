# Surface brief: MelodyMorph app (Console / Takes / Spec)

Mode: Operate (task: seed -> generate -> audition -> rate), demo-showcase audience. Code-led build (no image generation available). Free redesign confirmed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The interface is a concert poster that is also an instrument. The category default is a dark hardware console with neon mint LEDs; this refuses it for a Josef Mueller-Brockmann / Musica Viva poster world where the melody itself draws the artwork.

OWN-WORLD: One stark saturated field (vermilion-orange poster ground with ink-black and warm white planes; a cool ultramarine used only for the playhead and grid), faint visible construction-grid hairlines, huge lowercase grotesk wordmark "melodymorph" cropped by the viewport edge, concentric arc fragments whose radii/sweeps are generated from each melody's pitch and duration (the logo is the same arc system), small neutral grotesk for data, tabular numerals for measurements, stamped state words (FRESH, STALE, VOID). Recognisable with all content removed: arcs on a grid on a flat colour field.

STORY: The viewer understands a Transformer writes the next notes; sees the seed become arcs, presses RUN, watches takes assemble arc by arc, hears them, and trusts the controls because the grid and the stamped states are always honest.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left two-thirds: the wordmark at poster scale over the live seed-arc artwork on a visible 16th-step grid, seed piano roll beneath with a grid-reveal toggle; right third: a flat white control sheet (seed input, mode, dials, RUN) pinned in view. Top edge: three page links as plain lowercase text with a stamped takes count. Primary action RUN sits bottom-right of the control sheet in ink black.

FORM: Position 3 of the ordered list (player-piano roll, tracker, Musica Viva poster, Treatise, Blue Note, engraved score, console); seed key 47b1f279. Raises: "reveal construction" grid toggle (Crouwel); stamped states (jet-age ticket wallet).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
